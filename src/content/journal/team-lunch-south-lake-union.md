Feeding a team is easier when nobody has to pick an entrée. At Meat Moot, the table shares boards of smoked meat, with sauces, salads and drinks included, so ordering for eight takes about as long as ordering for two. Here's how to plan it.

## Book ahead for groups of six or more

We're at 400 Fairview Ave N, on the corner of Republican St, a short walk from the South Lake Union offices. Walk-ins are welcome when the room allows. For groups of **six or more**, call us at **(206) 261-0661** or [send an event enquiry](/events#enquire) and we'll have a table and carvers ready when you arrive.

## How much to order

Plan on about **½ lb of meat per person** for lunch and **½–¾ lb** for dinner. For a team of ten, that's roughly 5–7 lb, which works well split across two or three cuts:

- **Crowd-pleaser:** [beef brisket](/menu/beef-brisket)
- **The showpiece:** [beef ribs](/menu/beef-ribs) or [lamb ribs](/menu/lamb-ribs)
- **For the adventurous:** [lamb neck](/menu/lamb-neck) or [lamb shank](/menu/lamb-shank)

Everything comes with unlimited house sauces, salads, sides and soft drinks, so there's no separate drinks tab to split.

## Use the weekday lunch special

Monday to Friday, 12–3pm, weekday lunch is **$19.99** for a chicken quarter, ½ lb of smoked brisket, or one lamb shank. It's a predictable per-head cost for team lunches and expense reports.

## Everyone can eat

Meat Moot is **100% halal and alcohol-free**, so it works for teams with mixed dietary needs without anyone having to ask for a special menu. [Learn about our halal standards](/halal).

## Can't come to us?

We also cater. Tell us your headcount and timing and we'll smoke enough for the whole office. [Request a catering quote](/events#catering).
